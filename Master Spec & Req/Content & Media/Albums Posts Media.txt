ALBUMS / POSTS / MEDIA

Albums: title, description, slug, creator, created/updated metadata, visibility ও related media as defined।

Posts: title/content, creator, dates, visibility, ordering ও related media।

Media: image/video records, visibility, album relation ও display order where applicable।

Album gallery is a grouping/filtering mechanism. Album ↔ Post এবং Album ↔ Media relationship-এর actual database form audit করে CONFIRMED করতে হবে।

Hidden media public rendering-এ দেখানো যাবে না।
